# Morph Law

## What Morph Is

Morph is not decoration. Morph is locomotion.

Moving from one state to another is not a jump between pages. It is the same pixels relocating along a path you can see. The surface never leaves. The content rearranges. What feels like navigation is really the pixels sliding to new positions under one verb: **rebind**.

## The Range Is the Frame

Morph does not claim the whole world. It claims the screen you are looking at. That boundary is the selling point, because inside that frame every pixel is accountable. Nothing teleports. Nothing gets lost between states. The family trade is simple: you get instant, visible, reversible movement inside a space you can see, and you never pay for a reload you did not ask for.

## One Mechanism for Everything

Color, shape, label, position, and media type are all live bindings on the same surface. Mutate any binding and the surface re-renders from the new value. The pixel morph law is the same law applied everywhere:

- Same pixels, new coordinates.
- No flicker. No page swap.
- The frame is always the same surface. It just re-resolves.

This is the ACT surface binding law, locked in Automata at `automata/doctrine/act-surface-binding-law.md`. Define That's relabel pass is that same law applied to language files. Motifs, colored words, and dynamic short codes feed the bindings. Change the word, the binding updates, the interface re-renders.

## The Shift from Waiting to Acquiring

The goal is not to remove load time. The goal is to improve workflow by reducing load time and replacing it with actionable acquisition time. The user is not waiting for a page. The user is acquiring the next state the moment it becomes available.

## Optimus Instant

Optimus Instant is the product that carries this law. It collapses the three parents into one surface:

- **Ajax** gave the delta — instant partial updates, no full reload.
- **Go** gave the path — the single-binary spine the delta travels on.
- **Rust** gave the safety — memory-safe compute with no garbage-collector pauses on the hot path.

Validity is not a filter after the fact. The state is valid by construction. The moment the morph completes, the new state is there and usable. Zero dead time between what was and what is.

## The Full Range of Media

Static to readable to playable. Three media types, one verb, no seams:

- Press a button and the photo dissolves into its own text content.
- Press again and a video is on the screen, ready to play.

The smoothness is the point, because any stutter breaks the illusion that nothing ever left the screen.

## The Capability

As a capability, morph is a named, bounded promise: given a surface and a set of bindings, mutate any binding and the surface re-renders without reload. Inputs are the surface handle, the binding key, and the new value. Output is the re-rendered frame. The constraint is the pixel morph law. The witness is the before-and-after frame hash.

One verb: **rebind**.

## The Chromatophore Connection

The chromatophore is the biological precedent. A cell that changes color and pattern without changing its body. The nonagon is the stamp — eight tentacles plus the head, four corners, four mid-edges, one center. The octopus is the animal. The brain is the proof that many channels can converge on one integration point and produce a single accessible experience.

Morph is chromatophore in software. Same body. New form. The shape stays fixed. The values change.

## What This Is Not

This is not a page-transition library. This is not an animation framework bolted onto navigation. This is the means of movement itself — the way the system goes from one point to another without ever leaving the screen the user is watching.
