# Philosophy Chromatophore

## What This Is

This repository is the philosophy pool. It holds the doctrine behind the Actor Profile system: the octopus, the nonagon, the chromatophore, the brain as precedent, and Morph Law as the means of movement.

It was built through direct conversation. The questions asked and the answers given about the nine-node lattice, neural integration mechanisms, Global Neuronal Workspace Theory, Global Workspace Theory in AI, and the pure Go + Rust + Ajax base (no AI) are the contact material. That contact lives in the actor repository. This repository is where the philosophy itself is held.

## The Three Pillars

**Octopus** — eight tentacles plus one head. Eight are the load-bearing channels. The head is the fusion point. The octopus is the animal assigned to the Actor Profile itself. Every actor gets an animal. An insect is an animal. The animal is not decoration — it is the identity layer.

**Nonagon** — the nine-sided stamp. Not a claim that anything has exactly nine parts. The fixed geometry that can be placed on actors, cassettes, or brains. Nine nodes are enough to form any actor or cassette because the center is the birth cell and the eight tentacles are the load-bearing edges.

**Chromatophore** — the form. In biology, a chromatophore is a cell that changes color or pattern. Here it is the surface that takes shape according to the nonagon. The same nine nodes can appear with different values, different colors, different content. The shape stays fixed. The form changes.

## The Brain as Precedent

The brain is the biological precedent for the same pattern. Many channels (tentacles) converge on one integration point (head / center). The nonagon is how we stamp that pattern. The chromatophore is how the pattern takes visible form. Through the way we take shape with the nonagon, the way we take form with the chromatophore, and the way the brain works, we take precedent.

The brain solves distributed-into-unitary through thalamocortical loops, global neuronal workspace ignition, rate-based binding, and dendritic nonlinearities. The nine-node lattice captures that same pattern: eight load-bearing channels feeding one fusion point. The center is where local processing becomes accessible content.

## The Nine-Node Arrangement (Chromatophore)

Nine notes. Three on each line of the corner:

- One note at each of the four corners
- One note at the center of each of the four lines between the corners
- One note in the exact center

The corners are rounded, not sharp. It reads as a rounded square, not a hard-edged polygon.

```
        N
   NW ------ NE
   |          |
W  |    C     |  E
   |          |
   SW ------ SE
        S
```

Four corners. Four mid-edge notes. One center. The lines that connect the corners pass through the mid-edge notes, giving the rounded-square reading.

## The Symbiotic Event

The center cell (C) is the symbiotic event. It is not one of the parents. It is the child that only exists because the parts fused. In the octopus, the head is not one tentacle. It is what the eight tentacles make possible.

In Optimus, the symbiotic event is the moment Ajax, Go, and Rust combine. Ajax supplies the breath. Go supplies the spine. Rust supplies the compute. The center is the fusion that only exists because the three met.

## The Nine Nodes Can Form Anything

Because every actor and every cassette is stamped with the same nine positions, the lattice can hold any combination of traits. The eight surrounding nodes carry the parts (skills, rules, parents, dependencies, outputs, data sources, views). The center holds the fusion. The shape does not change. The values do.

## The Pure Base (No AI)

Go is the spine. Rust is the compute. Ajax is the surface. Together: the moment a change is valid, it is on the screen. Deterministic, inspectable, fast enough to feel continuous. This is the cassette that mounts on the ACT V4 canvas. It does not think. It moves.

What only a person can do stays outside: intent, judgment under incomplete rules, responsibility, taste, continuity of self. These are the center cell when the actor is a person. The eight tentacles can be automated. The fusion point is not.

AI sits above the pure base and beside the person. It fills gaps the pure system cannot see. It proposes the next micro-slice. It holds the lattice values. It never owns the center.

## Morph Law

Morph Law is the verb of this philosophy. Rebind, never teleport. The chromatophore is the skin — the surface that takes form. Morph Law is the muscle — the means by which that form changes without the body ever leaving the screen. Same pixels, new coordinates. One verb: rebind.

See `morph-law.md` for the full doctrine: the range-is-the-frame boundary, the Optimus Instant stack (Ajax, Go, Rust), the approved technology catalog, and the capability definition with before-and-after frame hash as witness.
