# AN-006 — Future comparative animal candidates

**Status: UNRESEARCHED IN THIS CATALOG.** Fish, amphibians and other reptiles were proposed as categories for future comparative studies. They are not evidence that particular species were part of the original founder discussion.

To add a species, create its own stable record with: exact name and species, original discussion provenance, observed color/structural mechanism, peer-reviewed sources, any change in physical state, any optical viewing dependence, inputs, outputs, uncertainty, potential software relevance, and proof status.

Candidate families to research, **not accepted findings:** reef fish and cephalopod mimics; color-changing frogs; anoles and geckos. Do not group unlike mechanisms as one verified fact.
