# AN-003 — Cuttlefish

**Current research state:** comparative cephalopod stub; species-specific acceptance not yet commissioned.

**Biology.** Cuttlefish are noted for dynamic body patterns used in camouflage and signaling. Pigment chromatophore organs work alongside other optical and skin structures; changing pattern in response to surroundings is a controlled biological effect.

**Key distinction:** the animal's real pattern can change while it remains the same organism. That differs from changing perceived color solely because the viewer moves.

**Measurements to seek:** background properties, visual stimulus, chosen skin pattern, response time, local reflectance, environmental light and viewer position.

**Chromatophore learning:** a coherent system can match several channels of outward expression to measured context, but matching a background is a design target, not security or concealment proof.
