# AN-001 — Octopus

**Current research state:** mechanism-level teaching stub; species and experiment acceptance not yet specified.

**Biology.** Octopus skin patterns can change rapidly through neural control of pigment-bearing chromatophore organs. Reflective cells and other skin structures can alter the total appearance; some octopuses can also change surface texture using muscular structures. Skin expression is reversible and is not the same process as developmental metamorphosis.

**Distinguish:** the existing [octopus.md](../../../octopus.md) assigns eight channels and one fusion point as a *system architecture analogy*. A biological octopus is not proof that an ACT actor literally has nine functional nodes or eight arms as computers use channels.

**Variables to measure:** stimulus, location on skin, latency, area, pattern, brightness, change of texture, recovery state, viewing and lighting conditions.

**Potential Chromatophore use:** responsive multi-part expression controlled by a stable owner, with explicit permission and observed state changes, not just a visual animation.
