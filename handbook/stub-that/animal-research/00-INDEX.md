### Required classification
- **Observed biology:** species-specific, source-backed mechanism.
- **Scientific uncertainty:** unknown, debated, species-specific, or not tested.
- **Chromatophore inspiration:** a candidate software design analogy, **not** a biological fact.
- **Implementation proof:** a separate authorized software test, not conferred by the catalog.

One animal can have several mechanisms. Never treat all animal color change as one type of chromatophore or assume an appearance shift means the object's physical identity changed.
