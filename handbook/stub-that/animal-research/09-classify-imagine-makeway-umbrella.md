**Do not collapse or discard characteristics:** an item can contribute multiple distinct traits. A perception-based illusion counts as *an observed appearance*, even when the object's underlying state is unchanged; it is not proof of physical transformation.

## Find the umbrella tip by interview—not by guessing

1. **Preserve** the raw source, named spoke, attributes, provenance, source classification, and unresolved facts.
2. **Classify** multiple traits independently: actual physical change / viewing-based illusion / scale / location / form / behavior / underlying state / identity and continuity. Compare mechanisms rather than treating all examples as equal.
3. **Interview** the founder about the common goal. Ask one focused question at a time; quote interpretations back for correction. Do not claim the tip has been found before explicit confirmation.
4. **Research** species-specific mechanisms and possible equivalent technologies; separate observed reality, qualified inference, and imagination.
5. **Imagine** several diverse, realistic ways to realize a confirmed aim with existing components, not new unrelated products.
6. **House of Cards** interrogates each proposed concept and its evidence; HOLD missing safety, owner, form or Function fields.
7. **MakeWAY** receives an admitted *candidate with verified input requirements*, not arbitrary free speech, and produces a read-only inventory/manifest/sequence proposal. StubTHAT and MicroSlice owners retain their own contracts.
8. **Prototype and test** with the required five-language real-hardware gate before selecting a runtime. Protected effects go through approval. Call it working only after independent receipts.

### What the founder's "Random" control means

**Random must stay on purpose.** Once the tip is confirmed, draw different *real-world examples or mechanisms* that demonstrably satisfy the tip's criteria, while preserving source, applicability, explanation and novelty. A random result is **not a random invented fact**. Provide a clear reselect control, track what was already shown and let the founder reject a weak fit. Never use a randomized suggestion to redefine confirmed intent.

**Until founder confirmation:** proposed tip = `UNRESOLVED`; random generation can only show clearly labeled discovery candidates, not accepted concepts or executable orders.

## MakeWAY starter order for the butterfly spoke (source example)

**What we know:** some butterfly wing scales produce iridescent effects through interaction with light; appearance can vary with viewer/light geometry even while the wing remains unchanged.

**What we have not chosen:** whether the desired software result is a viewer-angle-sensitive visual surface, a teaching lens, a general transformation engine or something else.

| LEGO block | Input | Result / HOLD |
|---|---|---|
| SOURCE | AN-005 butterfly record | Immutable record and source citation |
| CLASSIFY | Physical structure, optical conditions, visible outcome | Distinct classification labels, not merged into "changes color" |
| INVESTIGATE | Research source + user goal | Mechanism evidence and unanswered questions |
| IMAGINE | Confirmed tip and evidence | Candidate software behaviors with Function and Form |
| REVIEW | House of Cards | Qualified candidate or HOLD |
| STUB | Exact candidate + accepted owner/inputs/outputs | StubTHAT catalog entry and version |
| MAKEWAY | Reviewed stub + existing components | Dependencies, selected Way, construction/test manifest |
| SEQUENCE | Qualified plan | Dependency order owned by SequenceTHAT |
| CHECK | Explicit permission, tests and witness | Verified behavior or failure receipt |

### Testable teaching acceptance

- Every named spoke is visible; source truth and fictional examples are distinguished.
- Butterfly optical effect and metamorphosis remain two distinct traits.
- No record asserts that optical illusion is a physical change.
- Unresolved umbrella tip blocks MakeWAY *execution*; read-only planning is allowed.
- Generated candidates must cite their source and explain how they match **confirmed** intent.
- Test logs and real-device measurements are required before any implementation is called working.
