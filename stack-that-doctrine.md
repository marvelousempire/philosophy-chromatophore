# stack-that Doctrine

## What stack-that Is

stack-that is the engine room of the spaceship. It is the mitochondrion — it pumps energy through bonds. It does not render. It holds the catalog, the provenance, the release bindings, and it feeds the ACT V4 surface what it needs to morph.

## The Icon: Bubbles

The octopus is the animal for the Actor Profile. stack-that's icon is bubbles — the nonagon as a bubble. Big bubbles and little bubbles. Cells bonding. Ionic bonding. Covalent bonding. Nine sizes of shapes, from extra small to three-X large. XS is the cell. Three-X large is the planet. Each size is a node on the nonagon.

The bubble-size ladder lives at `data/bubble-size-ladder.md`.

## The Farm

stack-that is a farm. The juices need to flow. We bubble off, egg off, and ribbon off parts of the stack so we can start tying bows and ribbons around everything — giving them names as certain kinds of stacks — and then packaging them up and selling them as goods.

Like WordPress uses the LAMP stack, we name and package our own stacks from the MorphLaw-approved parts: WebGPU, WebGL 2, PixiJS, Three.js, Babylon.js, GSAP. Each named stack is a product. Each product is a ribbon around a set of parts.

## The Juice-App Motif

A juice-app theme and motif. All the ingredients — all the different fruits — are the different parts of the stack. The juicer is the engine. The juice is the stack. Run it through Morph Law and deliver it.

Fruits: WebGPU, WebGL 2, PixiJS, Three.js, Babylon.js, GSAP.
Juicer: stack-that's engine and catalog.
Juice: the named, packaged, sellable stack.

## The Relationship to Morph Law

stack-that is the parts catalog. Morph Law is the law those parts deliver. Automata's ACT surface is where the bindings live. ACT V4 is the canvas. Define That is the language source. philosophy-chromatophore is the philosophy home.

stack-that does not render. It stocks. It proves. It releases.
